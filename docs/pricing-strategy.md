# akFISHinfo Pricing Strategy

## Overview
akFISHinfo launches with a three-tier subscription model designed to balance early adoption, ROI validation, and long-term sustainability. Pricing reflects the economics of commercial fishing operations where software costs are minimal relative to daily operational expenses (fuel, gear, maintenance).

## Pricing Tiers

| Plan | Monthly Rate | Commitment | Annual Cost |
|------|--------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Monthly** | $30/month | Flexible, cancel anytime | $360/year |
| **Quarterly** | $80/quarter | ~$26.67/month | $320/year |
| **Yearly** | $300/year | ~$25/month | $300/year |

## Strategy & Rationale

### Launch Price: $30/month
We're launching at $30/month—not because it's our long-term price, but because it's low enough for captains to try the tool risk-free during the active fishing season. This is our only window to gather real-world proof that akFISHinfo saves time, improves catch efficiency, or reduces operational cost.

At current fuel costs ($30+ per half-hour operation), the software is objectively cheap. But that logic only works if captains *believe in the value first*. Early adoption at $30 removes friction and builds our case studies.

### Future Pricing ($50+)
Once we have a season of data showing concrete ROI—hours saved, better catch locations, reduced idle time—we'll raise prices to $50–75/month for new customers. The tool will have earned it.

## Early Adopter Lock-In Policy

**The first 50 customers to subscribe lock in their rate permanently.**

This is a genuine thank-you to the captains willing to test an unproven tool and provide feedback. It's good business: they give us validation; we give them lifetime pricing protection. It also creates urgency without being manipulative—early adopters get real value, and we get the data we need to scale with confidence. Once the first 50 seats are filled, all new customers subscribe at the current market rate.

## Competitive Context
Commercial maritime software typically runs $20–100+/month depending on features (navigation, weather, catch tracking, compliance). At $30–50, akFISHinfo is positioned as accessible but serious—not a toy, not a luxury.
